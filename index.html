Jeongin Bae

Research Engineer · Qualcomm Korea, AI Research

Hi! I'm an AI Research Engineer at Qualcomm Korea, where I recently joined after spending over three years at NAVER Cloud. During my time at NAVER, I worked across the Hyperscale AI Efficiency and Efficient AI teams, where I developed a deep foundation in model optimization research. I received a Bachelor's degree in Computer Science and Electrical Engineering from KAIST. My work spans quantization, efficient attention, pruning, and distillation — but quantization is where I've spent by far the most time and energy, from PTQ and QAT to BCQ and KV cache optimization. I also have a strong foundation in distillation and pruning, and I'm always excited to explore new directions at the intersection of efficiency and model quality.
